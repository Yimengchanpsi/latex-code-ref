\section{Governing equation}

The temperature field $u(x,t)$ evolves according to the heat equation

\begin{equation}
  \frac{\partial u}{\partial t} = \alpha \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2},
  \label{eq:heat}
\end{equation}

where $\alpha$ is the thermal diffusivity.
Equation~\eqref{eq:heat} is discretised with an explicit forward-Euler
finite-difference scheme and implemented in \coderef{heat_diffusion_equation}.

The scheme is conditionally stable; the stability criterion
$r = \alpha\,\Delta t / \Delta x^2 \leq \tfrac{1}{2}$
is checked in \codereffile{stability_criterion}
(see \coderefline{stability_criterion}).

\section{Boundary conditions}

\paragraph{Dirichlet.}
Fixed values $u(0,t)=u_L$ and $u(L,t)=u_R$ are enforced in-place
(\coderef{dirichlet_boundary}).

\paragraph{Neumann.}
A prescribed flux $\partial u/\partial x = q$ is approximated with a
first-order one-sided difference and applied in
\coderef{neumann_boundary}.
